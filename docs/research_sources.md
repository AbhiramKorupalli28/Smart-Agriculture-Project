# Research & Data Sources

- **USDA Agricultural Production Census (1.20 GB Big Data Dataset)**:
  - Kaggle Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/noeyislearning/agricultural-production
  - Role: Multi-gigabyte distributed Apache Spark benchmarking, state & commodity aggregations, and high-throughput validation (3,193,989 records).
- **Indian Crop Prediction Dataset**:
  - Source: https://github.com/AbhishekKandoi/Crop-Yield-Prediction-based-on-Indian-Agriculture
  - Role: Yield regression, seasonal production analysis, state-wise historical land usage (49,999 records).
- **Soil & Environmental Crop Recommendation Dataset**:
  - Source: https://github.com/AbhishekKandoi/Crop-Yield-Prediction-based-on-Indian-Agriculture
  - Role: 22-class crop classification, Random Forest feature importance, 5-cluster K-Means soil segmentation (2,200 records).
- **CATCrops Sentinel-2 Multi-spectral Time Series**:
  - Kaggle Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/irtaremotesensing/catcrops-dataset
  - Companion Dataverse: https://dataverse.csuc.cat/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.34810/DATA2322
- **Tata-Cornell Institute (TCI) and ICRISAT District Database**:
  - Portal: https://tci.cornell.edu/?projects=district-level-database-for-indian-agriculture-and-allied-sectors
- **Government of India DES APY Reports**:
  - Portal: https://data.desagri.gov.in/website/crops-apy-report-web
- **FAOSTAT (Food and Agriculture Organization)**:
  - Portal: https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/
